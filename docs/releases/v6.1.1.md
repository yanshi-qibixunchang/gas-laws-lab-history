# v6.1.1

发布日期：2026-08-27

6.1.1 正式加入活塞振动法空气比热容比实验，并完成混合精修仪器模型、电源门控、引导顺序、三轮采集与计算流程的整体验收。

## 简体中文发布说明

### 活塞振动法完整实验

- 新增活塞振动法实验工作区，支持完整演示模式、完整引导模式和常规 3D 操作。
- 引导流程覆盖设置采样率与压力、三轮 80/70/60 mm 高度实验、平台调节、刻度确认、螺钉锁紧与松开、软管断开与磁吸重连、采集、下压释放、停止、保存、周期框选、拟合和结果计算。
- 演示模式可自动播放全部步骤，并在结束时自动关闭仪器电源。
- 实时曲线、数据记录、周期处理和离线计算衔接使用同一组受校验实验数据。

### 电源与严格引导顺序

- 将传感器电源和电脑采集软件电源合并为仪器上的按压式电源控制，补充按下、回弹、发光、呼吸高亮和引导框选效果。
- 关机时保留“实时数据”标题，内容区只显示“电源关闭”；采样参数、曲线和数据记录均不可用。开机后实时区亮起并允许输入与记录。
- 引导模式新增第一步开机和框选后的关机步骤。未完成当前步骤时，其他控件只播放被按到的视觉反馈，不改变底层物理状态，并提示“请先完成当前引导步骤。”
- 关闭电源后直接进入计算，不增加额外的“离线计算”按钮。
- 修复从 5.3.1 升级时旧版活塞振动文件因缺少后续新增的演示会话字段而被安全隔离的问题；升级后会补入默认会话并保留原文件名称、身份和工作区位置。

### 混合精修模型与交互校准

- 正式模型保留项目当前版本的全部功能节点、层级、原点、坐标轴和运动契约，同时移植并精修 v1.3 主机界面与数据线视觉子树。
- 完成活塞、平台、玻璃筒、保护架、锁紧螺钉、软管、接头、刻度和读数节点的静态契约审计。
- 在真实浏览器中复核两种窗口尺寸以及正面、斜侧、普通和聚焦视角下的命中区域、圆周拖动、磁吸位置、高亮和相机焦点。
- 模型来源、基础资产、v1.3.0 来源、最终 SHA-256、精修范围和权利状态已写入随软件分发的来源记录和许可材料。

### 数据、兼容性与质量门禁

- 数值输入、半偶舍入、周期选择、线性拟合、空气材料参数和活塞非线性运动模型使用明确的校验边界。
- 保持既有应用 ID、产品名、安装器身份、更新仓库和用户资料目录不变，并继续使用版本化工作区迁移与损坏快照回退机制。
- 修复发行审计发现的依赖安全问题：`js-yaml` 升至 4.3.1、Electron 升至 41.10.7、electron-builder 升至 26.15.3，并同步更新 PostCSS、安装器工具和相关传递依赖；完整 npm 审计为 0 漏洞。
- 完整运行 TypeScript、全部自动测试、第三方许可生成校验、生产构建、NSIS 安装器与更新资产一致性检查。

## English release notes

### Complete piston-oscillation experiment

- Added the piston-oscillation method with a complete Demo, a complete guided workflow, and normal 3D operation.
- Guide covers acquisition settings, three 80/70/60 mm runs, platform and scale adjustment, screw locking and release, hose disconnect and magnetic reconnection, acquisition, displacement and release, stopping, saving, period selection, fitting, and final calculation.
- Demo automatically plays every step and powers the instrument off at completion.
- Realtime traces, recorded samples, period processing, and the offline calculation handoff share one validated experiment record.

### Power-gated acquisition and strict Guide sequencing

- Combined the physical sensor and computer-acquisition power state into a pressable instrument control with travel, rebound, illumination, breathing emphasis, and guided selection feedback.
- When powered off, the Realtime Data title remains while its body shows only “Power off”; parameters, plots, and recording are unavailable until power is on.
- Guide now starts with power-on and adds power-off after period selection. Out-of-step controls animate to acknowledge the press but cannot change physical state, and the app asks the learner to complete the current guided step first.
- Power-off transitions directly to calculation without an extra offline-calculation button.
- Fixed migration of piston-oscillation files created by 5.3.1: the upgrader now supplies the later default Demo session while preserving the original file identity, name, and workspace placement.

### Refined model, calibrated interaction, and release safety

- Preserved the project-current functional node, hierarchy, origin, axis, and motion contracts while transplanting and refining the v1.3 universal-interface and data-cable visual subtree.
- Audited piston, platform, cylinder, frame, screw, hose, connector, scale, and reading nodes and verified real-browser hit regions, circular screw gestures, magnetic snap, highlights, and camera targets at two window sizes and multiple views.
- Recorded the base asset, v1.3.0 source, final SHA-256, refinement scope, and rights status in packaged provenance and legal materials.
- Updated `js-yaml` to 4.3.1, Electron to 41.10.7, electron-builder to 26.15.3, plus PostCSS, installer tooling, and affected transitive packages; the complete npm audit now reports zero vulnerabilities.
- Kept the existing app ID, product name, installer identity, updater repository, and user-data path, then ran TypeScript, the complete automated suite, legal generation checks, production build, NSIS packaging, and update-asset verification.
