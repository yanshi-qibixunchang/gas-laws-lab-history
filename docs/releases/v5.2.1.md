# v5.2.1

发布日期：2026-07-25

5.2.1 完成 Gas Laws Lab 的首次使用与启动体验升级，加入三语言产品介绍和学习选择、统一工程软件提示体系，并让桌面开屏准确反映真实工作区恢复状态。

## 中文发布说明

### 首次使用与产品介绍

- 首次启动依次完成软件语言选择、欢迎、产品介绍、绝热膨胀法实验熟悉度选择和许可确认。许可版本变化时只重新确认许可，不重复前面的产品介绍。
- 三页动态内容分别展示实验工作区的建立，演示、引导、自由三种学习方式，以及计算核验与过程回顾。
- 产品介绍支持自动轮播、暂停、连续翻页、三语言、明暗主题和系统“减少动态效果”偏好；录制结构独立保留，后续界面变化时可以重新生成演示素材。

### 教程与提示交互

- 不熟悉本软件绝热膨胀法实验操作逻辑的用户会进入专用教程文件，依次完成演示与引导后解锁全部实验模式。
- 教程期间旧实验文件不会丢失，实验相关的新建、打开、关闭和导出操作受到控制；设置中提供退出与重新开始学习流程的入口。
- 浏览器中的原教程窗口关闭或失效后，新窗口可以安全接管学习会话，不再陷入必须返回不存在窗口的循环。
- 确认、告知、强制任务、Toast、持续警报和 Tooltip 使用统一的工程软件视觉、状态语义与键盘规则。

### 开屏与工作区恢复

- 原黑色“正在恢复工作区”占位页替换为与欢迎页一致的开屏动画。
- 进度由存储准备、读取、迁移、恢复和工作台就绪等真实阶段驱动；工作区尚未完成时最多停留在 99%，并显示等待旋转标识。
- 工作区真正绘制后才达到 100% 并进入软件。恢复失败时可重试或安全进入，错误界面不再使用左侧竖线样式。

### 修复

- 修复持久化投影中未定义字段导致有效工作区规范化失败的问题；浏览器预览和桌面正式构建共用相同生产路径。
- 恢复探索状态下的显微可视化开关，同时保持探索状态不记录正式实验数据。

## English release notes

### First-run experience and product introduction

- First launch now proceeds through app-language selection, welcome, product introduction, adiabatic-expansion familiarity, and legal consent. A changed legal version repeats only consent.
- Three animated pages demonstrate workspace creation, Demo, Guide, and Free learning modes, calculation verification, and Process Review.
- The introduction supports autoplay, pause, queued navigation, three languages, light and dark themes, and the system reduced-motion preference. Its capture structure remains available for regenerating media after future UI changes.

### Tutorial and prompt interactions

- Users unfamiliar with the app's adiabatic-expansion workflow enter a dedicated tutorial file and unlock all experiment modes after completing Demo and Guide.
- Existing experiment files remain safe while experiment-related create, open, close, and export actions are controlled during learning. Settings provides explicit exit and restart entries.
- A browser window can reclaim a stale tutorial session after the original window closes, eliminating the missing-window lockout loop.
- Confirmations, notices, forced tasks, toasts, persistent alerts, and tooltips now share one engineering-software visual language, state model, and keyboard policy.

### Startup and workspace restoration

- The old black workspace-restoration placeholder is replaced by the welcome animation.
- Progress is driven by real storage preparation, reading, migration, restoration, and workbench-ready stages. It waits at 99% with a spinner while restoration is incomplete.
- The startup reaches 100% only after the workbench is painted. Failures offer retry or safe entry without the former left-edge accent treatment.

### Fixes

- Fixed valid workspace normalization failures caused by undefined fields in persistence projection. Browser preview and desktop production builds use the same production path.
- Restored the microscopic visualization switch in Explore state without starting formal experiment recording.
